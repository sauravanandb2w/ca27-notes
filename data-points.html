Stray dogs — the 2025 Supreme Court "judicial flip"

Trigger: death of a child, Chavi Sharma, from rabies, 25 July 2025. 11 Aug 2025: two-judge bench ordered permanent shelter relocation of strays (Delhi/NCR). 22 Aug 2025: three-judge bench (Justices Vikram Nath, Sandeep Mehta, N.V. Anjaria) stayed this, reinstating the CNVR (Catch-Neuter-Vaccinate-Release) model pan-India.
India: ~33–36% of global rabies deaths (WHO). Rabies case load (IDSP): 1.5 million (2019) → 3.7 million (2024). National sterilization coverage: only ~30%. CNVR cost: ~₹1,500/dog one-time vs. ₹2,000–25,500/dog/month for shelter confinement.

Key term — vacuum effect: removing sterilized dogs from a territory lets unvaccinated animals migrate in, worsening (not improving) the public health risk.

Right to be forgotten

Jorawar Singh Mundy vs. Union of India (Delhi HC, 2021, Justice Pratibha M. Singh): US citizen acquitted of NDPS charges in 2011; sought de-indexing of the judgment from Google/Indian Kanoon/vLex due to ongoing reputational harm. Recognized right to be forgotten as part of Article 21 privacy.

DNA testing & paternity

Legal basis: presumption of legitimacy — Section 112, Evidence Act, 1872 (now Section 116, BSA). Child born during valid marriage presumed legitimate unless "non-access" is proven.

Gautam Kundu vs. State of West Bengal (1993): DNA test cannot be ordered as a matter of routine; prima facie non-access must be shown first.
Kamti Devi vs. Poshi Ram (2001): even genuine DNA evidence cannot automatically override the Section 112 presumption — needs "strong, clear, satisfying and conclusive evidence," not DNA alone. A more conservative position than a clean "two-stage test" giving DNA automatic preference.

Right to protest — constitutional basis & case law

Right to protest: a derived/composite right from Article 19(1)(a)/(b)/(c), read with Article 19(1)(d) (movement) and Article 21. Golden triangle: Articles 14, 19, 21. Restrictions under Article 19(2)/(3) must be minimum and pass the 3-prong proportionality test (legitimate state interest, rational nexus, balancing).
