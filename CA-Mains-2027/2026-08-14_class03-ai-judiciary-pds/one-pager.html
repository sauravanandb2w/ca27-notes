Topic 1 — AI in Judiciary

  • Human-in-the-loop is the standing conclusion: AI stays assistive; a human verifies and decides.
  • Pyramid of use — Red (prohibited): adjudication, bail, witness credibility. Amber (caution, verify): research, translation, transcription. Green (allowed): case management, scheduling.
  • Draft Regulations for Use of AI in Courts, 2026 (SC AI Committee, 3 June 2026): five principles — human primacy, transparency/explainability, fairness/non-discrimination, accountability, inclusivity/accessibility.
  • Institutional structure: full-time Apex Body at the SC + CORE-AI (Centre of Research and Excellence on AI) + Ethical Impact Assessment before deployment.
  • Regulation 43(3): mandatory disclosure by lawyers of AI use in pleadings/documents/evidence.
  • Black-box AI: opaque, unauditable reasoning. Accountability void: no single party (developer/judge/lawyer) answerable when AI-assisted decisions go wrong.
  • Algorithmic bias risk: AI trained on historical data (e.g., colonial "Criminal Tribes" classification) can reproduce old discrimination in bail/risk-scoring.
  • Domestic tools: SUVAS (SC translation AI, 36,000+ judgments, ~19 languages); Kerala's 24×7 ONCOURTS (Kollam, cheque-bounce e-court); SUPACE (SC research-assistance tool, distinct from SUVAS).
  • Global comparators: China piloting AI for petty-case decisions; US strict-sanctions regime post-Mata v. Avianca.

Topic 2 — PDS / SARTHAK-PDS

  • Three generations of food security: Availability (Green Revolution) → Affordability/Accessibility (Revamped/Targeted PDS) → Sustainability (climate-smart agri, PDS modernization).
  • Institutional roles: FCI — procurement/storage (centralized vs. decentralized); State — ration cards, FPS, intra-state movement; FPS dealer — last-mile distribution.
  • CAP model (Cover & Plinth): open-air grain storage when covered capacity is short — major source of wastage, often wrongly blamed only on rodents.
  • SARTHAK-PDS (Cabinet-approved 27 May 2026, ₹25,530 cr, FY26–31): merges intra-state movement/FPS-margin assistance with SMART-PDS; covers 81.35 cr NFSA beneficiaries.
  • Three AI modules: NIRMAL (beneficiary registry), ASHA (grievance redressal), SAKSHAM (supply-chain/vehicle tracking).
  • Digitization stats: ~99.8% ration cards Aadhaar-seeded; ~99.6% FPS ePOS-enabled; One Nation One Ration Card for portability.
  • Scholarly debate: Shanta Kumar Committee (Jan 2015) wanted NFSA coverage cut 67%→40%; Himanshu & Sen opposed — leakage rose ~24%→~54% (1999-00 to 2004-05) as coverage narrowed, so favored near-universal coverage.
  • Significance beyond food security: consumption-equalizer (vs. cash transfers), crisis buffer (COVID/grain shocks), farmer income support via MSP procurement.
FCI (procurement) → State (allocation/movement, SAKSHAM tracking) → District → Block → FPS (NIRMAL registry, EPOS+Aadhaar) → Beneficiary (ONORC portability, ASHA grievance redress)

Must-know case law & data

NCLT/NCLAT "methyl isocyanate" case (SC, 2 July 2026, Narasimha & Aradhe JJ.) — set aside orders in J&K Bank vs. Essel Infraprojects (₹87.43 cr default) after tribunals relied on six hallucinated AI precedents; SC likened it to a Bhopal-style toxic release: "invisible, insidious, and catastrophic by the time anyone notices." Mandated zero tolerance; BCI to frame AI guidelines.

ICRIER leakage data: ~28-29% of PDS grain (FCI + states) doesn't reach intended beneficiaries — ₹69,108 cr loss (~20 million tonnes), based on FCI offtake vs. HCES data, Aug 2022–Jul 2023.
