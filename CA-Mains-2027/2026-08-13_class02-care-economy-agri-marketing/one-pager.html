Care Economy & Unpaid Domestic Work

  • Care economy = all paid + unpaid activities caring for people/household; unpaid care work invisibilized in GDP/census.
  • Until 2011 Census, homemakers classified as non-workers (with beggars, prisoners).
  • Time poverty → vicious cycle: poverty → caregiving burden → less time → low productivity → fewer opportunities → poverty.
  • Motherhood penalty (exit on childbirth) vs. marriage penalty (exit on marriage alone, "soft patriarchy").
  • Double burden/second shift: paid work + unpaid domestic work simultaneously.
  • Diane Elson's 3R: Recognize, Reduce, Redistribute → basis of SDG 5.4.
  • Claudia Goldin: U-shaped LFP curve; "greedy jobs" explain the "sticky floor."
  • Robin West: "intimate labour"; Veblen: leisure class / "tradwife" critique.
  • Constitutional pegs: Art. 15 (substantive justice), Art. 21 (dignity), Art. 39(a)/39(e)/42 DPSP.
  • Best practices: MGNREGA creches, Palna scheme, Jal Jeevan Mission (reduce), Sweden (paternity leave + subsidized creches/geriatric care).
Must-know case: Shishupal @ Shish Ram vs. Surjeet (SC, 11 June 2026) — new "loss of domestic care" head, ₹30,000/month minimum notional income for homemakers; "homemakers are nation builders." Also: Madras HC (21 June 2023) — "a 24-hour unpaid job cannot rank below an 8-hour paid job," equal share in husband's assets.
Must-know data: NSO 2019 — women spend ~7.3× more time than men on unpaid work (19.8% of day vs. 2.7%); valued at ~3.1–5% of GDP (verified range; lecture's "15–17%" is an outlier estimate). IMF: GDP could grow ~27% if female LFP = male LFP.

Agricultural Marketing

  • India = 7th largest agri-exporter (~₹5 lakh crore/yr exports; ~US$55bn globally, 20% to West Asia alone — concentration risk).
  • Agriculture = state subject; APMC/mandi system regulates market, functions (auctions), functionaries (arhatiyas).
  • eNAM (launched 2016): 1,656 mandis, 23 states + 4 UTs (Mar 2026); 1.80 cr+ farmers; trade value ₹3.19 lakh cr (2024) → ₹4.84 lakh cr (2026).
  • Peri-Urban Vegetable Clusters: new ₹2,000 cr Central Sector Scheme, clusters within ~100 km of cities.
  • Upstream bottlenecks: low yield (apple 9-10 t/ha vs NZ 40 t/ha), MSP-driven cereal-centric cropping, land fragmentation.
  • Downstream bottlenecks: post-harvest loss ~₹90,000 cr/yr; <10% processing; 67–82% of fruit sold via middlemen (FICCI); cold-chain meets <15% of need (ECA-linked investment risk).
  • Bihar case study: abolished APMC (2006) without alternative investment → marketing ecosystem collapse — cautionary tale against full deregulation.
  • Way forward: calibrated/incremental liberalization + FPO aggregation, not wholesale deregulation.
  • Best practice models: Sahyadri Farms (grapes, women's employment), Kalanamak rice (GI + FPO + traceability), quick-commerce (farmer→FPO→dark store→consumer).
  • Govt tools: Bharat Vistaar (AI mandi-rate platform), Agri Infrastructure Fund, negotiable warehouse receipts.
