Topic 1 — MMDR Amendment Act 2026

  • Fiscal federalism test case: minerals are nationally regulated (Entry 54) but locally born (Entries 49/50).
  • Mineral rights = 3 legal aspects: mining activity, mineral (ore), mineral-bearing land (MBL).
  • Case-law arc: India Cement (1989) royalty=TAX → Kesoram (2004) royalty≠TAX → MADA (2024, 9-judge, 8:1) royalty≠TAX confirmed, states keep Entry 49/50 power + retro dues allowed.
  • MMDR Amendment 2026, Section 9D: bars state tax/cess/levy on mineral rights/MBL; Section 9D(2) nullifies uncollected past dues (paid dues not refunded) — "rewards non-payers."
  • DMF (Sec 9B): 10%/30% of royalty, welfare of mining-affected districts. NMET (Sec 9C): ~2% of royalty, funds exploration.
  • Laffer curve: optimum tax ~35-40%; India's effective mining tax rate is ~50-58%, in the "prohibitive" zone → capital flight, illegal mining, import substitution.
  • Debates: cooperative federalism vs. "legislative imperialism"; fiscal certainty (industry) vs. fiscal autonomy (states); levy maximization (Jharkhand, backfired) vs. production maximization (Odisha).
  • Counter-point: ~90-95% of mining money still flows to the state; Centre's own share is mainly the 2% NMET cut.
  • Way forward: GST-Council-style consultative rate mechanism; GST-style compensation grant for revenue-losing states; Ghana/Canada benefit-sharing models.
Must-know: MADA (2024)
  • 9-judge SC bench, 8:1 (Nagarathna J. dissenting); overruled India Cement (1989).
  • Held: royalty is not a tax; states retain Entry 49/50 taxing power, subject to Entry 54 limits.
  • Retro dues capped: recoverable only from 1 April 2005, staggered over 12 years from 1 April 2026.

Topic 2 — SCO @ 25 / Bishkek Declaration

  • 26th SCO Summit, Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (31 Aug–1 Sep 2026), 25 years since 2001 founding; theme "Together for Sustainable Peace, Development and Prosperity."
  • Origins: "Shanghai Five" → China-led, Eurasian, intergovernmental; objectives: mutual trust, "three evils" (terrorism/separatism/extremism) via RATS (Tashkent), economic/energy/cultural cooperation.
  • 10 members: India & Pakistan (2017), Iran (2023, 9th), Belarus (2024, 10th). ~40% world population, ~25% land mass/GDP.
  • Key terms: multi-alignment (India's diversification strategy); asymmetric multilateralism (formally equal, actually China-dominated); "tarmac-and-steel" (China, infra-led, BRI) vs. rule-based multilateralism (West).
  • "Virus of conflict": India-China/Pakistan/Iran-Pakistan bilateral tensions; Russia-Ukraine & Iran-Israel spillover; anti-West (China) vs. non-West (India) framing; talking-shop critique.
  • India's 3 pillars (PM Modi, Bishkek 2026): Security, Connectivity, Opportunity — "vision" to "concrete outcomes."
  • Bishkek outcomes: English made an official SCO language (with Russian, Mandarin); new "Universal Center" for threats beyond RATS (narcotics, cyber, trafficking); chairmanship passed to Pakistan.
  • SCO vs. QUAD: Eurasian/land/China-led vs. Indo-Pacific/maritime/rule-based — India's dual membership = multi-alignment in action.
Must-know: numbers & firsts
  • Founded 2001; India+Pakistan full members 2017 (Astana); Iran 2023 (9th); Belarus 2024 (10th).
  • ~40% world population, ~24-25% land mass, ~20-25% global GDP, ~20% of oil/energy reserves.
  • 2026 Bishkek: English added as official language; Universal Center approved; chair → Pakistan (next summit).
  • Intro hook for any grouping question: link it to an India FP label — SCO = multi-alignment/extended-neighbourhood; BRICS = Global South leadership; SAARC/BIMSTEC = Neighbourhood First.
  • Safe generic conclusion case (any resource/sustainability answer): M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath (1996) — Public Trust Doctrine, state as trustee for present & future generations.
