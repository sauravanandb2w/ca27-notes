High-yield facts

  • Feb 28, 2026: US-Israel strike on Iran (Op. Rising Lion / Epic Fury); Khamenei reportedly killed.
  • Iran blocks Strait shipping in response — "commerce chokepoint" → "instrument of coercion."
  • Carter Doctrine (Jan 23, 1980): US pledge to defend Gulf oil by force — replaced now by transactional Trump Doctrine ("ROI or no war").
  • UNCLOS: Iran signed but never ratified — claims weaker "innocent passage" (Art. 19) over "transit passage" (Art. 38).
  • Strait: ~21 nautical miles wide; carries ~20% of world's liquid petroleum.
  • India: ~40–50% of crude oil imports and ~50–60% of LNG imports transit this Strait.
  • India's "Triple Squeeze": energy crisis + fertilizer crisis (Oman ~45% of India's urea) + consumer price crisis.
  • "Two Blue Water Challenge": Malacca Strait (east, China-linked) + Hormuz (west) — maritime version of the two-front problem.
  • Chabahar Port (India 10-yr contract, May 13, 2024): outside the Strait, anchors INSTC, counters China's Gwadar.
  • Quotable doctrines: Samson Option (Iran), Pottery Barn Rule (Colin Powell), BANI world (Jamais Cascio, 2020).

Must-know callouts

Data point — CareEdge Global / IEA A sustained US$10/barrel oil price rise widens India's CAD by 0.3–0.4% of GDP and cuts real GDP growth by 0.2–0.5 pp. IEA ranks India among the top-3 nations most exposed to a Middle East energy shock (with South Korea, Thailand).
Legal nuance — UNCLOS Transit passage (Art. 38): commerce-first, continuous, cannot be suspended. Innocent passage (Art. 19): sovereignty-first, can be suspended. Iran's preference for the latter is a live, unsettled legal dispute — good exam nuance.
