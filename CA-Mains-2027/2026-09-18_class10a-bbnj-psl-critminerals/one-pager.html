Exam-critical — do not forget
  • BBNJ in force 2026 (60 ratifications, 120-day rule) — but MARPOL already existed before it, so BBNJ ≠ "first ocean pollution treaty," it's the first high-seas biodiversity conservation treaty.
  • PSL sub-targets are each % of total base (ANBC/CEOBSE), never nested: agri 18%, NCF 14%, SMF 10% — not 14% of 18%, not 10% of 14%.
  • Critical minerals numbering: 30 (committee list) → 24 (Part D, statutory) → 17 (rare earths, one combined Part D entry). Coking coal added to Part D, 29 Jan 2026.

2. BBNJ "High Seas Treaty"

GS1/GS2 · Mains: Moderate
  • Adopted June 2023; 3rd UNCLOS implementation pact (after Fish Stocks Agreement, Deep Seabed Mining pact).
  • 60 ratifications reached 19 Sep 2025 → in force 17 Jan 2026.
  • India signed, not ratified; Sanjay Upadhyay Committee drafting domestic law.
  • Excludes warships/military aircraft/commercial fishing; covers MPAs, genetic-resource benefit-sharing, EIAs, precautionary principle.

3. Ship-pollution treaty map

GS3 (Environment) · Prelims: High
  • MARPOL (IMO, London): routine ship pollution — oil, chemicals, sewage, garbage, air (6 annexes; only I & II mandatory).
  • Ballast Water Mgmt Conv.: invasive species via ballast water.
  • OPRC 1990: oil-spill preparedness/response.
  • Hong Kong Conv. 2009: ship recycling/breaking.

4. RBI Priority Sector Lending

GS3 (Economy) · Prelims: High
  • Base = higher of ANBC or CEOBSE.
  • Targets: domestic banks 40% · RRBs 75% · SFBs 60% (cut from 75%, FY25-26) · UCBs 60%.
  • Sub-targets (all % of base): agri 18%, NCF 14%, SMF 10%, micro-ent. 7.5%, weaker sections 12% (15% for RRBs).
  • PSLC trades compliance only, not the loan; shortfall parked in RIDF (NABARD) at penal sub-market interest.

6. Coking coal & MMDR Part D

GS3 (Resources) · Mains: High
  • Coking coal → critical/strategic mineral, 29 Jan 2026 notification; added to Schedule I Part A & Part D.
  • India resources ~37.37 billion tonnes (not million); imports ~58 MT (FY24-25); Jharkhand-heavy.
  • 30 (Dermal Cmte. list) → 24 (Part D) [23 from the 30 + glauconite] → 7 outside Part D: antimony, bismuth, copper, germanium, hafnium, silicon, strontium.
  • 6 atomic minerals reclassified to Part D (2023 Act): Li, Ti, Nb, Ta, Zr, Be — opens private mining.

7. Greenland geopolitics

GS1/GS2 · Prelims: High (map-based)
  • Kingdom of Denmark; outside EU (Greenlanders still Danish/EU nationals).
  • US interest: military basing, rare earths/minerals, Arctic security; UN Charter Art. 2 (sovereignty) invoked against any annexation talk.
  • Chokepoint chain: Labrador Sea → Davis Strait → Baffin Bay / Denmark Strait → GIUK Gap (Cold War Soviet-sub route).
