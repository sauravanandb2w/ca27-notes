Topic 1 — Exam Integrity (2026 Amendment Act)

  • Offense: cognizable, non-bailable, non-compoundable
  • Punishment: 5-10 yrs jail; fine up to ₹50 lakh; debarment 8 yrs (service providers)
  • Investigation must close within 2 months; Special Fast Track Courts + Special Public Prosecutors
  • 42nd Amendment (1976) moved education to Concurrent List; Art. 21A (RTE), Art. 45 & 46 (DPSP)
  • Centralization (NEET/UGC-NET/UPSC) vs. decentralization debate — uniform standards vs. local flexibility
  • Cobra effect: harder exams to curb cheating risk making exams more elite, not fairer
  • Radhakrishnan Committee (2024, post-NEET leak): 101 recommendations — two-stage NEET (JEE-style), shift-based testing above ~2 lakh candidates, cap on NEET attempts
  • Proposed 50:50 board-exam/entrance-score weightage model (attribution unverified — cite as "a Ministry of Education committee has proposed")
  • China's Double Reduction policy — cuts homework & tuition burden (grades 1-9), transferable idea
  • Answer-writing tip: give suggestion first, attribute after; conclude via social-mobility bridge

Topic 2 — MSME Sector

  • MSME share: ~31% GDP, ~35% manufacturing output, ~46-48% exports, ~9 crore units, 40+ crore employed (PIB, class figures)
  • Twin classification: investment (plant/machinery or equipment) and turnover — both matter together, not investment alone
  • MSMED (Amendment) Act 2026: permanent statutory status for Udyam Portal; Online Dispute Resolution — 90d mediation, 30d to refer arbitration, 90d for award
  • Delayed payments: law mandates 45-day payment to MSEs; compliance weak; old disputes dragged ~2 years
  • TReDS: invoice discounting gets MSMEs paid in 1-2 days instead of waiting a buyer's cycle; mandatory for CPSEs as buyers
  • Dwarf firms (stay small for policy/compliance benefits) vs. gazelles (young, fast-growing, net job creators) vs. missing middle (too few mid-sized firms) vs. gazelle gap (India-specific shortfall)
  • Inverted duty structure: import duty on inputs > finished goods — discourages domestic manufacturing
  • India vs. China vs. Vietnam: e-commerce exports ~$5bn vs ~$300bn vs —; cost of capital 6.2-8.2% vs ~3.1% vs ~1%
  • Non-Tariff Barriers (TBT/SPS): EU/UK/Japan/China checks on chillies, tea, Basmati, shrimp, dairy etc.; costly voluntary certifications
  • MSMEs as inclusion tool: absorb rural youth/women, curb distress migration, sustain regional balance (handlooms, handicrafts)
