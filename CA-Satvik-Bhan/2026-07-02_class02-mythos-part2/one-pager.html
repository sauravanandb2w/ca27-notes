Class 2 — One-Pager: Mythos Part 2 (Sovereignty · Gaps · Steps · Way Forward)

UPSC GS-3 Internal Security / S&T + GS-2 IR — Satvik Bhan CA, 02-07-2026 (post-lunch half) — last-minute revision sheet
Full notes: notes.html · Static: ai-geopolitics-static.html · Part 1: Class 1 one-pager

1. Implications (rest)

  • AI sovereignty: frontier AI tools = tools of geopolitics; access decides geopolitical might. E.g. US restricted access to Fable & Mythos citing national security. Economic security > political security (geo-economics > geopolitics).
  • Cyber-power inequality: haves (Global North) vs have-nots (Global South, dependent on vendors). Machine-speed attacks, human-speed patching. Precedent: China → Tiangong after ISS ban.
  • AI cyber arms race: China's Qihoo 360 building Mythos-type tool.
  • Hybrid & asymmetric warfare: non-state actors need no costly weapons; target CI/air-defence software; Iran vs US (horizontal escalation, cheap drones).
  • Financial cost: MSMEs hit most (no capability, trained staff, backups, incident plans).
  • Conclusion: "Mythos-led frontier AI era is a national security concern."
Gap in time: US ~6 mo ahead; NY 6 mo behind Silicon Valley; frontier labs ~1–2 yrs ahead of India (lecturer's view).

2. Merits of access (flip side)

  • Better (sovereign) cyber capability · early access → indigenisation · capacity building · economic (shorter patch time, less manpower; 100 experts × ₹1 cr) · deterrence vs LeT/JeM-type ransomware.
  • Dual-use: NSG · MTCR · Australia Group · Wassenaar.

Chips & static hooks

  • ASML (NL) = only maker of EUV lithography; US told it not to supply China.
  • ISM: ~12 plants approved; ISM 2.0 due; equipment imported.
  • Rare earth corridors / sintered permanent magnets; critical minerals = Prelims 2025 (3 Qs), 2026 (1).
  • Mission Sudarshan Chakra (air defence, 15 Aug speech); check PIB text.

3. Gaps (A–F)

  • A Vision: cyber seen as support fn → need AI-enabled, autonomous, network-centric view.
  • B Institutions: AI Safety Institute headless (set up ~Oct 2025); no model-testing body (UK AISI, US CAISI have); IndiaAI Mission = development > safety; light-touch regulation (like Japan); many agencies, no convergence (State police · MeitY/CERT-In · MHA/I4C · NSCS).
  • C HR: exploit analysts, code reviewers, reverse engineers, cloud/API experts, legal-forensic; ~6 lakh gap; CERT-In 29.44 lakh incidents.
  • D Law: IT Act 2000; no liability for non-patching; no mandatory disclosure; no penalty for AI-exploit misuse; safe harbour (s.79).
  • E Classification & pre-release: risk-tiering; controlled access; jailbreak standards (US did this for Fable/Mythos).
  • F Other: R&D ~0.6% GDP; 2013 cyber policy; poor patching (WannaCry, AIIMS 2022); sector coordination; no AI-cyber playbooks/drills; MeitY budget.
Chain: Vision → Institutions → People → Law → Classification → Money.

4. Steps taken

  • High-level review: FinMin + MeitY + RBI, NPCI, CERT-In (not MHA).
  • RBI: advisories to banks/NBFCs; global central-bank coordination (Fed, BoE); boards approve gap assessment + time-bound plan.
  • Project Glasswing engagement for access.
  • Panel under SBI Chairman to assess AI-platform risk and mitigate.
  • Earlier: RBI 2025 framework for responsible, ethical AI in finance.
Review→RBI→Glasswing→SBI panel

5. Way forward

  • Grand strategy first.
  • Policy/legal: National Frontier AI Cyber-Security Strategy (AI-assisted vuln discovery, AI phishing, model access governance, incident reporting); National AI-Cyber Fusion Centre (CERT-In, NCIIPC, I4C, RBI, SEBI, DoT, firms, clouds; cf. IFC-IOR Gurugram).
  • Sectoral: continuous AI-assisted audits; mandatory asset inventory; sector AI-cyber drills; small entities (co-op banks, MSMEs); trusted vendor list.
  • Regulation: laws must not weaken cyber security (Digital Competition Bill: side-loading).
  • AI diplomacy: Quad, G20, BIMSTEC, ASEAN; India–Israel.
  • Skilling: industry–academia–govt.
  • Sovereign AI: domestic models; models above threshold → international notification & review (IDSA); Indian Glasswing-like cohort (Sarvam, Hanooman); pre-release trials.
  • Whole-of-government: national security inseparable from national digital resilience.
