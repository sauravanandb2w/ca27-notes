2. Concept & functions

  • Cyber security = protection of cyber space: computer systems · networks · data · digital services (UPI) · critical infrastructure.
  • From: unauthorised access · disruption · manipulation · espionage · fraud · destruction.
  • Functions (IBM): protect sensitive data · prevent threats · business continuity · secure digital infra · maintain CIA · secure communication · regulatory compliance (GDPR 2018; DPDP Act 2023) · identify & mitigate risk · educate users (cyber hygiene) · incident response.
  • CIA: Confidentiality (authorised only) · Integrity (not altered) · Availability (when needed).
  • Hygiene: 2FA · password change · antivirus · backups.

3. Elements / pillars (7)

  • Network (firewall, encryption) · Information (CIA, physical + digital) · Endpoint (antivirus) · Cloud · Application (secure code, auth) · Operational (risk mgmt, workflows) · DR & business continuity (backup, IRP).

Types of attack

  • Malware (virus, trojan, spyware, bots; Android God Mode) · Ransomware (double extortion: exfiltrate + threaten to publish) · Social engineering (phishing, vishing=voice, smishing=SMS, spear=VIPs) · DDoS · MITM (rogue Wi-Fi, DNS spoofing) · Zero-day · Supply chain (vendor, software, cloud, hardware: Hezbollah pagers) · others: account takeover, defacement, server compromise.

4. Attack vs crime

  • Almost all attacks are crimes; not vice versa.
  • Attack = technical, on a system; govt term "cyber security incident" → report to CERT-In (MeitY).
  • Crime = unlawful act (law decides), computer = target / tool / place; police/I4C (MHA); NCRB data; IT Act 2000 + BNS.
  • Exception: ethical hacking (attack, not crime). Crimes not attacks: digital arrest, stalking, bullying, piracy, betting, deepfake.
  • I4C 4 categories: crypto crime · cyber terrorism · hacking · online financial fraud.
Lecturer's view: AI-enabled attack is more dangerous than AI-enabled crime (removes technical barrier). WormGPT / FaaS: low entry barrier.

5. Cyber crimes list

  • Cyber attacks · online financial fraud (KYC, job, WFH, loan app, matrimonial/romance) · digital arrest · blackmail / sextortion · stalking, bullying, harassment, doxing · CSEM (NCPCR) · fake news (mis / dis-information), deepfakes · piracy, IP · online betting/gaming (Online Gaming Act 2025) · crypto fraud (WazirX ~US$230 mn) · cyber-enabled trafficking, scam compounds.
  • Digital arrest = not a legal term; impersonate police/CBI/ED/customs/TRAI/court; claim money laundering / drugs / illegal parcel; keep on video call (psychological control). CBI: Operation Chakra.
  • Telegram: whole platform blocked (Sec 69A) after paper leaks — post → account → platform.

6. Evolution, definitions & facts

  • Evolution (box): cyber crime → cyber fraud + human trafficking + transnational organised crime + armed-group funding. Example: scam compounds (Karen State, Myanmar–Thailand; Cambodia; Laos; Golden Triangle). Single → double extortion; FaaS; AI phishing.
  • Cyber terrorism (IT Act) threatens sovereignty, integrity, security, public order (NIA case 2025). Cyber espionage = secret ops to steal strategic data. Cyber warfare = states/non-state actors disrupt, degrade, destroy, manipulate, spy; need defence + deterrence; cyberspace = 5th domain (US).
  • Vulnerable sectors: critical infra · finance · health · telecom.
  • Hotspots: Jamtara (Jharkhand) · Mewat/Nuh (Haryana) · Chandigarh (narco) · map them; quote source (MHA, MeitY).
  • Impact heads: economic · social/psychological · political (trust) · national security · geopolitical reputation.
