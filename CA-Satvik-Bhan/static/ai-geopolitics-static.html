3. Semiconductors and chip supply chain

ItemPoint
India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)Approved Dec 2021, outlay about ₹76,000 crore ★ (PLI-type fiscal support for fabs, display fabs, ATMP/OSAT). ~12 plants approved so far per the lecture; ISM 2.0 expected.
Key projects ★Tata Electronics–PSMC fab (Dholera, Gujarat) · Micron ATMP (Sanand) · Tata OSAT (Assam) · CG Power–Renesas OSAT.
ASMLDutch firm; only maker of EUV lithography machines. US pressed it not to export the latest tools to China. India depends on imports of such equipment.
TSMC / TaiwanLeading-edge contract fab; Taiwan's centrality is a geopolitical risk factor.
GPUsNvidia dominates AI GPUs; bulk AI orders by frontier labs divert fab capacity and raise prices of regular chips (lecturer's anecdote). India's IndiaAI compute pillar pools GPUs for startups and researchers.
TermsFabless · foundry · ATMP/OSAT · wafer · die · lithography · node (nm).

6. Warfare and defence hooks

  • Hybrid warfare: blend of conventional, irregular, cyber, information and economic means. Grey-zone: below the threshold of open war. Asymmetric: weaker party exploits strengths-weaknesses gap. Horizontal escalation: widening a conflict to new fronts/domains (Iran's strategy as per lecture).
  • Information warfare and narrative battles (Operation Sindoor discussion); decision latency; machine-assisted command.
  • Mission Sudarshan Chakra: national air-defence shield announced in the PM's 15 August speech ★ (target around 2035). Lecturer: runs on software; verify exact text on PIB.
  • Information Fusion Centre – Indian Ocean Region (IFC-IOR): Gurugram, run by the Indian Navy ★ — model for a proposed AI-Cyber Fusion Centre.
  • Past cyber-conflict precedents: Estonia 2007, Georgia 2008, Ukraine–Russia; Stuxnet (2010).
  • Pegasus: spyware by Israel's NSO Group; India–Israel cyber and defence cooperation.

8. IR hooks from the class

  • Geo-economics now dominates geopolitics: economic security decides sovereignty (supply chains, chips, minerals, AI access).
  • India chairs BRICS in 2026; France holds the G7 presidency — chance to bridge Global North and South; congruence with France on AI governance.
  • De-risking / China plus one / Indo-Pacific / Quad: background for Japan PM's India visit and India–Netherlands ties (ASML). Indians abroad: UK, Germany, Netherlands lead in Europe (lecturer; ~2.5 lakh in the Netherlands).
  • Dependence and tech denial examples: China — ISS exclusion → Tiangong; chip export controls (H200 partial relaxation).
  • India–Israel cyber and defence cooperation.
  • Myanmar scam compounds sit on the Thai border in Karen State under armed groups; India's border states are Arunachal, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram; Sittwe (Rakhine), Chin and Sagaing regions are the places India engages.
