1. IT Act, 2000 — sections to know

SectionSubject
43Damage to computer / system without permission (civil compensation) ★
66Computer-related offences (dishonest/fraudulent acts under s.43) ★
66CIdentity theft
66DCheating by personation using a computer resource (digital arrest, phishing fall here)
66EViolation of privacy ★
66FCyber terrorism
67 / 67A / 67BObscene material / sexually explicit / child sexual abuse material
69Interception, monitoring, decryption ★
69ABlocking of public access to information (Telegram, accounts, posts); upheld in Shreya Singhal (2015) with safeguards ★
70 / 70AProtected system; National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre (NCIIPC) ★
70BCERT-In as national agency for incident response ★
79Safe harbour for intermediaries (subject to due diligence); 79(3)(b) takedown on govt/agency notice
The IT Act has no definition of "cyber attack"; government documents use "cyber security incident" (CERT-In). "Digital arrest" is not a legal term.

7. Hygiene and key terms

  • CIA triad: confidentiality, integrity, availability.
  • Seven elements: network, information, endpoint, cloud, application, operational, DR/BCP.
  • Hygiene: 2FA, password change, antivirus, backups, avoid unauthorised sites, don't leave logins open.
  • Exfiltration = data leaving a system; infiltration = entry.
  • Misinformation (unintentional) vs disinformation (deliberate).
