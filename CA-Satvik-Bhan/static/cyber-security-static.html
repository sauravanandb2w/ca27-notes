4. Policy & initiatives

  • National Cyber Security Policy 2013 (replacement strategy long awaited ★).
  • Cyber Surakshit Bharat (MeitY, 2018) — CISO training/awareness.
  • Mandatory cyber audits for large/critical entities via CERT-In empanelled auditors.
  • Government cloud: MeghRaj; DPI: Aadhaar, UPI, CoWIN, DigiLocker, DigiYatra, PFMS.
  • Cyber crime reporting: cybercrime.gov.in; helpline 1930; financial fraud suspension system (CFCFRMS) ★.
  • IndiaAI Mission (compute, foundation models, safety) ★.

8. Warfare terms

  • Hybrid warfare: mix of conventional, irregular, cyber, information and economic means.
  • Grey-zone warfare: coercion below the threshold of open war.
  • Asymmetric warfare: weaker party exploits the stronger one's vulnerabilities.
  • Information warfare: narratives, disinformation, deepfakes.
  • Fifth domain: cyberspace (after land, sea, air, space).
  • Cyber deterrence: difficult due to attribution problem.

9. Tech terms

  • SCADA / ICS: control systems for power, water, manufacturing.
  • CBS: Core Banking Solution. RTGS/NEFT/UPI/IMPS: payment rails.
  • SBOM: Software Bill of Materials — list of components in software.
  • Red teaming: simulated attack to test defences. Bug bounty: reward for reporting flaws.
  • Encryption, 2FA, firewall, VPN, honeypot, sandbox.
  • Legacy software: old systems still in use; hard to patch.
