6. Federal angle

  • Police and public order are in the State List; the Centre coordinates (CBI, I4C, CERT-In, NCIIPC).
  • Police academies: central (SVP National Police Academy, Hyderabad) vs state academies — capability gap.
  • Cyber crime is borderless; jurisdiction and inter-state coordination are weak; Zero FIR helps but needs coordination.
